## Implications for our assessment

The proposed distinction is a project brief connected to transparent evidence: explain a family’s suggested role, distinguish reading needs from mood preferences, and show uncertainty when evidence is missing. This is a direction to test, not proof that other tools lack these features.

Keep the requested 1, 2, or 3 families as an explicit choice. Later experimentation should support real content and retaining a chosen family while changing another. A third family still needs a purpose. Treat size, weight, spacing, and color as part of testing the combination in context; a family name alone does not establish a successful hierarchy.

No reviewed page establishes that its recommendations satisfy our pilot reading tasks. We have not evaluated those products against the tasks, so no comparative performance claim is justified.
