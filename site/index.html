How this pilot works

How this pilot works

Required language and character coverage determine which candidates are available for each role. Your style choices and slider priorities then influence a small set of editorial rules.

The rules use documented design associations. They are not trained mood scores, measured readability ratings, or match percentages. This is not a search across the full Google Fonts catalogue.

Character support is checked against the supplied font files. That does not establish correct composition, reading comfort, or a successful cross-script pairing.
